## Project brief

**Colleagues** is a collaborative learning platform for groups who study the same material together. Whether you're grinding through coding interview prep, exploring a new tech topic, or running a team book club — Colleagues gives everyone a shared workspace to stay in sync, track progress, and keep the conversation going.

The moderator sets the direction. Everyone else follows along, checks off their work, and builds on each other's thinking.

---

## The problem

Group learning falls apart because there's no shared system. You're posting assignments in a chat thread, tracking progress in a spreadsheet, and scheduling sessions on a separate calendar — everything scattered. When you can't see who's keeping up and who's falling behind, accountability disappears.

Colleagues brings it all into one place.

---

## Features

### 📚 Concept of the Month

The whole group focuses on the same topic at the same time — a challenge series, a subject area, a curriculum. The moderator sets the current concept with a title, description, and timeframe, so everyone is aligned on what matters this month.

### 📋 Assignment Feed

A clean feed of problems and tasks posted by the moderator, each with a title, link, and optional notes. Members work through them at their own pace and check off what they've completed.

### 👥 Group Progress Overview

A shared grid of members and assignments that shows where everyone stands at a glance — and provides a natural nudge to keep moving.

### 🏆 Leaderboard

Full ranked standings scored by assignments completed and events attended. Filterable by month or all-time, and by concept track. The top three get a podium. Everyone sees where they rank.

### 📈 Progress & Activity

A personal analytics dashboard showing completion pace across daily and weekly views, curriculum completion rate, and total score. Access is gated behind a points threshold — you earn it by doing the work.

### 💬 Discussion Threads

Every assignment has a comment thread where members can share solutions, ask questions, or drop hints. The best learning happens in the conversation after the problem.

### 🗓️ Events

Moderators schedule group events — coding challenges, mock interviews, open study sessions. Members RSVP and everyone can see who's joining.

### 👤 Profiles

Each member has a profile with their name and activity. A simple presence for a focused community.
